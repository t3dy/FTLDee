John Dee (1527–1608/09)

Biographical events for FTLDee encounter authoring. Source: research/DEE_MASTER_BIOGRAPHY.md. All claims are tagged by grounding status.

ATTESTED Named source + page range — the game may state it.   CONTEXT General history; usable.   DISPUTED Scholarship actively disagrees — write the dispute.   LEGEND Dee's self-fashioning or later tradition.   COUNTERFACTUAL Departure from record; named scholarly licence.

Act I — Formation (1527–1551)

  • 13 July 1527 ATTESTED
    Born in London
    Father Rowland Dee, mercer and gentleman sewer at Henry VIII's court. Welsh royal descent from Rhodri the Great: LEGEND — Dee's own claim; mattered to his imperial self-fashioning.
    Parry 23–27; Harkness 26–28
  • 1542 ATTESTED
    St John's College, Cambridge
    Trained in logic, mathematics, and humanist disciplines. Eighteen hours of study per day — LEGEND (Dee's own account in the Compendious Rehearsal).
    Parry 29–35; Harkness 26–30
  • 1546 ATTESTED
    Founding Fellow of Trinity College
    Taught logic and sophistry in a university culture already dependent on patronage — Parry's first instance of the pattern that governs the whole life. The Pax scarab — a flying beetle engineered for an Aristophanes production: LEGEND.
    Parry 31–35
  • 1548–1550 ATTESTED
    Louvain — Gemma Frisius and Mercator
    Where mathematics and astrology become practical technologies of service (Parry ch. 2). Returns with instruments and globes. The game's starting skill set originates here.
    Parry 35–48; Harkness 28–30
  • 1550 ATTESTED
    Paris — Lectures on Euclid
    Continental reputation as mathematical teacher established. Overflowing hall and declined readership: LEGEND (Dee's own account).
    Harkness 28–30; Szőnyi

Act II — Suspicion (1552–1558)

  • 1555 ATTESTED
    Arrested — Calculing and Conjuring Charges
    Astrological work under Mary I draws political suspicion. George Ferrers as accuser; Secretary Bourne as interrogator. Cleared of treason; held on religion. Parry ch. 3: magical expertise is simultaneously useful and incriminating. The game's founding paradox.
    Parry 48–58
  • 1555 DISPUTED
    Remanded to Bishop Edmund Bonner's Household
    The nature of the association is disputed: collaboration, survival strategy, or genuine Marian conviction. Foxe's Acts and Monuments fixes an image early (36 corpus hits). The game writes the dispute; does not resolve it.
    Parry 48–58; Foxe

Act III — The Election (1558–1570)

  • 1558–1559 ATTESTED
    Consulted at Elizabeth's Accession; Coronation Date
    Astrological expertise becomes useful at the accession. The election of the coronation date. Transition from suspect calculator to potentially useful Elizabethan counsellor.
    Parry 48–58; 34 corpus hits
  • 1558 ATTESTED
    Propaedeumata Aphoristica
    Astrology rebuilt as a physics of rays. Szőnyi: this printed natural philosophy is already symbolic magic — so Act V is not a rupture.
    Harkness 98–102; Szőnyi; Håkansson 274–289
  • 1564 ATTESTED
    Monas Hieroglyphica
    Written at Antwerp in the house of royal typographer Willem Silvius. Dedicated to Maximilian II (104 hits). DISPUTED: narrowly alchemical (Parry), geometrical cabala (Walton), or disciplina noua in Pythagorean/Trithemian number symbolism (Clucas). Continental afterlife through Khunrath, Libavius, Rosicrucianism (Forshaw). M-K hinge: Ibn Turka's Ṭahawī Circle is "the Islamic answer to Dee's Hieroglyphic Monad."
    Harkness 76–90; Clucas Ambix 64.2; Forshaw
  • 1570 ATTESTED
    "Mathematicall Praeface" to Billingsley's Euclid
    Taxonomy of the mathematical arts addressed to craftsmen and mechanicians. Dee notes Murphyn's intensifying slanders — earliest hard date for the reputational threat.
    Sherman; Parry 100–105

Act IV — The Limits (1570–1582)

  • 1560s–1570s ATTESTED
    The Mortlake Library
    The greatest private collection in England. Sherman: a living working institution of acquisition, annotation, consultation, lending, and loss — not a monument. The game's Library screen.
    Sherman 56–80; ch. 4, 116–204 (marginalia)
  • 1576–1577 ATTESTED
    General and Rare Memorials and the Imperial Programme
    Navigation, maritime policy, British imperial recovery. Arthur and Madoc as prior title against the papal donation. DISPUTED: real maritime policy (Sherman) vs. occult ambition in political code — "more is hid, than uttered" (Parry).
    Sherman 277–394; Parry 114–138
  • c. 1560s–1582 ATTESTED
    Vincent Murphyn's Slander Campaign
    Forged letters in Dee's name; damaged prospects over two decades. Parry's thesis: reputation is a historical force, manufactured and weaponised. There is no clean resolution — the slander outlasts every rebuttal.
    Parry 87–109; 26 corpus hits
  • 1582 ATTESTED
    Calendar Reform — Dee Gives the Mathematics; the Bishops Refuse
    Mathematical correction inseparable from confessional and political caution. 147 corpus hits. Even technical expertise becomes religiously charged.
    Parry 170–183; Harkness 155–159

Act V — The Glass (1581–1583)

  • 22 December 1581 ATTESTED
    First Surviving Angelic Action — Barnabas Saul
    Saul fails and recants (119 corpus hits). Harkness's argument: the motive is not credulity — cabala, alchemy, and the end of Nature; an Adamic exegetical key needed before the eschaton.
    Harkness 35–42; Whitby
  • March 1582 ATTESTED
    Edward Kelley Enters as "Talbot"
    His criminal past: CONTEXT. Cropped ears: LEGEND. Jane Dee's warnings attested in kind (251 diary hits). Kelley's sincerity: DISPUTED — the corpus does not adjudicate it.
    Harkness 35–40; Parry 55–58; 74 hits for Talbot
  • 1582 ATTESTED
    The Book of Soyga and the Eastern Thread
    Put to the angels; Uriel defers; Michael named as expounder. 89 corpus hits. M-K (2021): the Soyga's lore derives from the Bunian-Bistamian magical corpus popular in Ottoman courtly circles. The Ottoman thread is present from the very first angelic sessions.
    M-K 2021; 89 corpus hits
  • 1582–1583 ATTESTED
    The Heptarchic System
    Tables, seals, the Sigillum Dei Aemeth. Visionary sessions becoming structured ritual technology of names, offices, prayers, and objects.
    Harkness 40–44; Håkansson 240–332; 167 hits

The Ottoman Route COUNTERFACTUAL — M-K 2021

"Ibn Turka is best approached as a Timurid Dr. Dee — or Dee best approached as an Elizabethan Dr. Littleturk." — Melvin-Koushki, "Dr Dee's Ottoman Adventure," Hellebore (2021)

The historical record: Dee's imperial bids ran through London and Prague; he never went east; he died poor. The licensed counterfactual: Constantinople and the court of Murad III (r. 1574–95) — Sufi initiate, devoted to oneiromancy, astrology, and talismanry, whose reign spanned the Islamic millennium (992 AH / 1592 CE). A court where Dee's exact profile was patronage-worthy rather than prosecutable.

The path is earned through specific choices that accumulate the ottoman_thread_open flag — starting with the Book of Soyga sessions. See research/OTTOMAN_CONNECTION.md and TurkaGame for the Ibn Turka parallel.
