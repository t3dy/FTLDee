FTLDee
A Historical Career Roguelite

You are John Dee, intellectual courtier, mathematician, astrologer, and natural philosopher. Navigate the courts, libraries, and intelligence networks of Elizabethan England c. 1580. Build your repertoire. Choose your patrons. Decide whether to stay or depart for the Continent.

About the Game

FTLDee is inspired by FTL: Faster Than Light — a node-based roguelite where you manage resources, make choices with incomplete information, and face escalating pressure. But instead of a spaceship, you manage a household and a library. Instead of weapons, you invest in books, skills, contacts, and patronage.

The core design equation is BOOK + SKILL + CONTACT + PATRON = OPERATION. Knowledge is combinatorial, not additive. Blue-outlined options — borrowed from FTL — unlock when you have the right combination of books and skills. The historiographical interpretation of Dee by Melvin-Koushki provides the frame: the grimoire is a manual for the courtier.

Every event in the game carries a historical status tag:

Dee's Career at a Glance

1527–1551
Formation
Cambridge, Louvain, Paris. Mathematics becomes practical technology. Instruments and globes acquired. Continental reputation established.
1552–1558
Suspicion
Arrested under Mary I. Calculing and conjuring charges. Bonner's household. Magical expertise is simultaneously useful and incriminating.
1558–1582
The Elizabethan Career
Mortlake library. Monas Hieroglyphica. Mathematical Preface. Calendar reform. Navigation counsel. Rivals. The reputation market.
1581–1583
The Glass
First angelic action. Edward Kelley enters. The Heptarchic system. Albert Łaski arrives. The continental question.
1583–1589
The Crossing
Departure with Łaski. Prague, Rudolf II. Třeboň, Rožmberk. The communion of wives crisis. Returns to England without Kelley.
1589–1609
The Petition
Manchester wardenship. The Apologetical Letter. Petition to James I. The library dispersed. Death, 1608 or 1609.

Game Features

Node Map
Six Elizabethan locations: Mortlake, Richmond, Greenwich, Windsor, Barn Elms, London — each with faction requirements and travel costs.
Blue Options
Encounter choices gated by specific book + skill + contact combinations. Visual design borrowed from FTL.
Eight Factions
Elizabeth, Burghley, Leicester, Walsingham, Religious Authorities, Scholar Network, Merchant Network, Continental Courts.
Ottoman Thread
A licensed counterfactual based on Melvin-Koushki's argument — the path east to Murad III's court, earned through specific choices.
Historical Status Tags
Every event tagged: DOCUMENTED, PLAUSIBLE, CONTESTED, COUNTERFACTUAL, or ANACHRONISTIC.
Seeded Runs
Mulberry32 seeded RNG. Identical seeds reproduce identical encounter sequences. Save and continue across sessions.
